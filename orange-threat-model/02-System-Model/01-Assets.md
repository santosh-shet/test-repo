# Assets

## Purpose

This document identifies the assets within the Orange Managed Infrastructure service that require protection. The assets include infrastructure components, hosted workloads, operational services, and business information supporting Tieto Banktech.

Asset identification establishes the foundation for trust boundary analysis, threat identification, and risk assessment.

---

# Asset Classification

Assets are categorized according to ownership and operational responsibility.

---

- Customer Data

# Security Considerations

The identified assets will be evaluated during the threat modelling process to determine:

- Critical assets requiring enhanced protection.
- Assets crossing trust boundaries.
- Assets exposed to external or internal threats.
- Security controls required to maintain confidentiality, integrity and availability.

# Next Steps

The identified assets will be used to:

- Identify trust boundaries.
- Define infrastructure data flows.
- Identify threat actors.
- Perform STRIDE analysis.
- Build the threat register.
- Assess risks and identify security controls.
