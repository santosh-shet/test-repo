# 06 – Risk Assessment

## Overview

The **Risk Assessment** section evaluates the risks identified during the threat modelling exercise for the Orange Infrastructure Services supporting Tieto Banktech applications.

Building upon the identified threats, this section assesses the likelihood and business impact of each risk, determines its priority, and defines the appropriate treatment strategy.

The objective is to support informed risk-based decision making and prioritize security improvements that reduce the overall risk to the hosted services.

---

# Objectives

The objectives of this section are to:

- Assess the likelihood and impact of identified risks.
- Prioritize risks based on their overall severity.
- Document risk owners and treatment decisions.
- Define consistent criteria for evaluating risk.
- Support risk acceptance and remediation planning.

# Risk Assessment Process

The risk assessment process consists of the following activities:

1. Identify threats.
2. Determine affected assets.
3. Assess likelihood.
4. Assess business impact.
5. Determine overall risk rating.
6. Identify existing controls.
7. Select an appropriate risk treatment.
8. Monitor and periodically review residual risk.

---

# Expected Outcomes

Upon completion of this section, readers should understand:

- Which risks pose the greatest threat to the environment.
- The likelihood and potential business impact of each identified risk.
- The priority assigned to each risk.
- The existing security controls that reduce risk.
- The selected treatment strategy for each identified risk.
- Any residual risks requiring ongoing monitoring or formal acceptance.

This information supports security planning, governance, compliance activities, and continuous improvement of the Orange Infrastructure Services supporting Tieto Banktech applications.
