# Glossary

This glossary defines commonly used terms throughout the Orange Infrastructure Services Threat Model.

---

# A

## Asset

Anything that has value to the organization and requires protection.

---

## Attack Surface

The collection of points where an attacker could attempt to compromise a system.

---

## Authentication

The process of verifying the identity of a user, device, or service.

---

## Authorization

The process of determining what an authenticated user or system is permitted to access.

---

# B

## Backup

A copy of data maintained to support recovery following data loss or system failure.

---

## Business Impact

The potential effect of a security incident on business operations.

---

# C

## CIA Triad

Information security principles consisting of:

- Confidentiality
- Integrity
- Availability

---

## Component

A logical or physical part of the system architecture.

---

## Confidentiality

Protection of information against unauthorized disclosure.

---

# D

## Data Flow

Movement of information between users, applications, infrastructure, or services.

---

## Denial of Service (DoS)

An attack intended to reduce or prevent system availability.

---

# I

## Identity Provider (IdP)

A service responsible for authenticating users and issuing identity assertions.

---

## Integrity

Protection against unauthorized modification of information.

---

# L

## Least Privilege

Granting only the permissions required to perform an assigned function.

---

# M

## MFA

Multi-Factor Authentication requiring two or more independent authentication factors.

---

## MITRE ATT&CK

A knowledge base describing adversary tactics and techniques observed in real-world attacks.

---

# P

## PAM

Privileged Access Management.

---

## PASTA

Process for Attack Simulation and Threat Analysis.

A seven-stage risk-centric threat modelling methodology.

---

## PKI

Public Key Infrastructure used for managing digital certificates and cryptographic keys.

---

# R

## RBAC

Role-Based Access Control.

---

## Residual Risk

Risk remaining after security controls have been implemented.

---

## Risk

The combination of the likelihood of an event and its potential business impact.

---

# S

## SIEM

Security Information and Event Management.

---

## STRIDE

A threat modelling methodology categorizing threats into:

- Spoofing
- Tampering
- Repudiation
- Information Disclosure
- Denial of Service
- Elevation of Privilege

---

## Security Control

A safeguard implemented to reduce security risk.

---

# T

## Threat

A potential event capable of exploiting a vulnerability and causing harm.

---

## Threat Actor

An individual, group, or organization capable of causing a security incident.

---

## Threat Model

A structured representation of potential threats affecting a system.

---

## Trust Boundary

A logical point where data, users, systems, or administrative control cross between different trust levels.

---

# V

## Vulnerability

A weakness that may be exploited by a threat actor.
